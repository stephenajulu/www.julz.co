      <div class="container container--md">
        <h2>Our Core Values</h2>
        <p>
          These represent our unchanging commitments — the core standards of character and responsibility that define our identity:
        </p>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Excellence</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We reject the culture of the hurried, minimum viable product. We believe in high craft, rigorous testing, clean typography, and structural integrity. Every detail, from a hidden database query to the tactile layout of a physical device, is executed to a premium, civilization-grade standard.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Compassion</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            Technology should serve human focus, dignity, and sanity. We design tools that respect your attention, do not mine your psychological vulnerabilities for advertising revenue, and provide quiet, utility-first value without triggering digital noise.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Stewardship</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We hold technical resources and knowledge in trust. We recognize our responsibility to the soil, local communities, and future generations. Our designs prioritize resource conservation, biophilic integration, and ecological longevity.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Sovereignty</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We build tools that promote self-reliance, local custody, and data ownership. We design for offline-first resilience, personal homelabs, and private AI. We believe individuals and communities should own their infrastructure rather than rent it.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Craft & Beauty</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            Aesthetic harmony is a technical requirement, not a secondary veneer. We draw from architectural proportions, natural structures, and clean minimalism. Technical artifacts should be satisfying to interact with, inspiring a sense of wonder and permanence.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Generational Focus</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We design for endurance. Our products, software configurations, and documentation are built to outlive their creators. We structure our systems to run independently of central corporation APIs, ensuring longevity that spans decades.
          </div>
        </details>

        <br>
        <h2>Our Operating Principles</h2>
        <p>
          These are the active coordinates and operational parameters that dictate how we explore, build, and rest:
        </p>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Curiosity Leads</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We ask questions that stand outside standard industrial assumptions. We explore coordinates boldly, guided by possibilities rather than current market conveniences. Independent research is our default compass.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Imagination Fuels</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We draw creative energy from sci-fi design, biblical covenants, historical crafts, and childhood wonders. We build systems that make these imaginative horizons technically feasible and functional.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Responsibility Grounds</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We build with an awareness of our duty to God, human dignity, community structures, and the Earth. Responsibility ensures that our prototypes remain constructive, ethical, and biophilically safe.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Transparency Defines</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            Our software is open, our designs are auditable, and our processes build trust. Building in the open allows our work to remain transparent, inviting public review, independent audit, and communal trust.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Progress Empowers</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We forge technical solutions that restore individual agency and human capability. True progress does not automate away human skill; it expands it, providing tools that encourage self-reliance.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Sabbath Sustains</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We honor natural cycles of rest. We step away from the screen and the workbench to sustain physical, creative, and spiritual life. Sabbath is our active defense against burn-out and frantic acceleration.
          </div>
        </details>

        <details class="expandable-card">
          <summary>Craft Beauty</summary>
          <div class="details-content">
            We prioritize aesthetic harmony, tactile design, and architectural proportion in every digital and physical form. Beautiful design commands respect, evokes care, and contributes to the longevity of the object.
          </div>
        </details>
