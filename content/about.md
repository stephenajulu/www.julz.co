        <h2>The Core Narrative</h2>
        <p>
          JULZ exists to transform raw ambition into enduring technical artifacts. Founded in Kenya in 2016 by Stephen Ajulu and established as a sovereign venture in 2023, the forge is built on a single conviction: that technology should support human focus, individual sovereignty, and biophilic balance.
        </p>
        <p>
          We do not build for quick speculative exits, nor do we compromise our engineering standards to satisfy venture-capital milestones. Our timeline is long-term, and our focus is on technical self-reliance. From local-first offline databases to modular battery management systems, every venture is forged with clear covenant responsibility.
        </p>

        <h2>The Founder</h2>
        <p>
          <strong>Stephen Ajulu</strong> is the sole systems builder, technologist, and brand strategist behind JULZ. Fusing ICT infrastructure, modular branding, and local prototyping workflows, he acts as the singular coordinator of the forge. Stephen operates JULZ as a personal crucible, demonstrating that a focused independent builder can design and ship civilization-grade tools that outlast their creators.
        </p>
